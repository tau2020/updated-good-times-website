## Results in detail

- **Unit and integration** (`npm test`, node:test, PGlite and workerd), at `511cbe4`: **400 tests, 400 pass**.
- **Browser**, locally at `511cbe4` (`PW_WEBKIT=1 npx playwright test`): **390 passed, 49 skipped, 1 failed**. The one
  failure is `motion.spec.mjs:73` (the aircraft for a visitor arriving past the hero), which fails on untouched `main`
  too. Its cause, found on the cloud: `/#gallery` starts a smooth scroll that ScrollTrigger's scroll restoration
  interrupts at DOMContentLoaded and again at load, so the page settles 103 px down, under the 450 px at which the hero
  finishes its entrance at once; the full entrance takes 8.1 s against the test's 5 s. Nothing in this work touches it.
- **Static QA** (`npm run check`): 27 pages, no problems; every page under 200 KB (the home page 186 KB: it draws eight
  review cards and reads the rest from `/reviews/` on the first Load more).
- **Words over the backdrops** (`qa/scenic-contrast.mjs`): every line over every plane meets AA against its worst
  decile at 1440 and 390 px, the tightest the large green "None of the worry." at 3.05:1 (3:1 needed). Since every former
  leaf became the standard green pill, the tool also reads two pills in those bands (Get a Quote, Arrange My Transfer):
  their white words measure 5.0 to 5.1:1 on average and 4.5:1 at the pill's own top-left gleam (`--btn-sheen`), at the AA
  line. That is the standard pill everywhere on the site, not the backdrop; softening the gleam (18% to 12%) would lift it
  everywhere, and is the owner's call as a change to every button.
- **Performance and 200% zoom**: as measured before the follow-ups (CLS 0 to 0.01; no horizontal scrolling at 640 and
  720 px CSS width); the cloud lane re-measured CLS on the five pages with backdrops.
- **Console**: no errors and no failed requests on the pages checked, locally and on the deployments.
