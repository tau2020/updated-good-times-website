# Rights register

Origin, authority and status of every photograph, the logo, reviews, prices and business facts the site uses. Checked on 5 Nov 2026. A photograph is added here before it is used; one whose status changes to "withdrawn" is removed from `src/data/images.mjs` and its renditions deleted.

Being in Drive does not, on its own, make a photograph usable. Each one below has a recorded basis, and the points the owner still has to confirm are in `docs/APPROVAL_REGISTER.md` (P1–P3, P5, P7–P9). The media audit of 6 Nov 2026 (`audit/`) added the photographs in its own table below, retired others, and made each photograph appear in one place only.
